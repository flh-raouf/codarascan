Course

Deterministic Barcode Localization

A short course for finding barcode boxes in scanned industrial car manufacturing documents before decoding them.

1. Visual Census

Learn the page types and traps in the dossier.

2. What A Barcode Is

Understand bars, spaces, modules, and scan lines.

3. Barcode Formats

Separate visual families from encoded meanings.

4. QR And 2D Codes

Learn why matrix codes need a different detector.

5. Detection vs Decoding

Keep localization and reading cleanly separated.

6. Preprocessing

Prepare scanned pages without destroying barcode signal.

7. 1D Localization

Build the classical detector idea.

8. False Positives

Reject tables, text, and scanner streaks.

9. Library Lab

Use OpenCV, ZXing, and pyzbar intelligently.

10. Build Detector

Turn the idea into a dossier pipeline.

11. Evaluate

Measure boxes, not vibes.

12. Decode Crops

Rectify boxes and try decoders.

13. Research Checkpoint

Decide if learning-based detection is justified.
