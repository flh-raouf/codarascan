Lesson 9

Library Survey Lab

Use libraries as probes. Do not surrender the problem to them.

LibraryUseful forWatch out
OpenCV BarcodeDetector1D detection and decoding with quadrangle outputs.May miss rotated or low-resolution dossier labels.
OpenCV QRCodeDetectorQR-specific detection and decoding.Not for ordinary 1D labels.
pyzbar/ZBarDecoding 1D and QR codes, returning rectangles/polygons.Needs native ZBar on macOS/Linux.
ZXingStrong multi-format decoding and format support.Detection behavior depends on wrapper and image quality.

Lab idea

Run each library on page 16, page 20, and page 21. Record three things only: found boxes, decoded strings, and misses. That tells us whether the library is a detector, decoder, or both for our pages.

Retrieval check

What is the best first use of these libraries?

  1. Compare behavior on real pages.
  2. Assume they solve everything.

Primary sources: OpenCV BarcodeDetector, ZXing, and pyzbar.
