Lesson 6

Preprocessing Scanned Pages

Preprocessing should make barcode texture easier to see without erasing thin bars.

Useful operations

  • Render at 200-300 DPI: enough pixels per bar without making everything slow.
  • Grayscale: barcode signal is mostly intensity, not color.
  • Local contrast: useful when scans have shadows or uneven paper.
  • Threshold: creates dark foreground from light paper.
  • Morphology: closes barcode bars into one candidate blob.

Rule of thumb

If a preprocessing step makes the thin bars merge beyond recognition, it may still help localization but hurt decoding. Save intermediate debug images.

Retrieval check

Why not render at very low DPI?

  1. The PDF title changes.
  2. Small bars lose detail.
