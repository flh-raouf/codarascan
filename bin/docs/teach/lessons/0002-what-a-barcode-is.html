Lesson 2

What A Barcode Is

A 1D barcode is a controlled pattern of dark bars and light spaces. A scanner reads it by sampling across the bars.

The mental model

Imagine drawing a horizontal line through the barcode. Along that line, pixel intensity jumps dark-light-dark-light many times. The widths of those runs carry data. This is why barcode detection is often a texture problem before it is a decoding problem.

white margin | black bar | white space | black bar | ... | white margin
quiet zone     encoded symbol                          quiet zone

Why it stands out

  • Many nearby, parallel edges.
  • Large contrast between dark bars and white paper.
  • Compact rectangular shape.
  • Many transitions along one direction, but not the perpendicular direction.

Retrieval check

For a 1D barcode, what does one scan line mainly measure?

  1. Runs of dark and light widths.
  2. The printed text under the symbol.
