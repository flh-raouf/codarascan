Lesson 13

Research Checkpoint

Only consider training after deterministic failure is measured and named.

Decision rule

Classical detection is enough if the remaining misses are rare and explainable by small tuning changes. A learned detector becomes reasonable if failures are varied, frequent, and expensive to encode as rules.

EvidenceNext move
Mostly misses rotated labels at known angles.Add angles or improve box merging.
Mostly false positives on tables.Improve transition and quiet-zone filters.
Labels are blurred or damaged.Improve rendering, denoising, and crop decoding.
Many visual cases with no clean rule.Use ground truth to train or benchmark a detector.

Literature path

Read classical morphology work first, then the BarBeR benchmark survey. That gives you the map before choosing tools.
