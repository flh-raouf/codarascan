Lesson 1

Visual Census Of The Dossier

Before writing code, learn the image world your detector must survive.

The 21 pages include empty pages, header labels, pasted labels, rotated labels, dense tables, handwriting, and scanner streaks.

The useful categories

  • Header barcodes: mostly horizontal and repeated near the top.
  • Pasted labels: smaller, often rotated, sometimes touching table lines.
  • Hard negatives: tables, vertical scanner streaks, dense text, signatures, and highlighter marks.
  • 2D codes: square-ish matrix symbols, visually different from 1D bars.

Do this

Open page 16 and page 21 from the contact sheet. Count only the barcode-like regions you would ask a detector to box. Ignore plain printed numbers.

Retrieval check

Which observation matters most before designing the detector?

  1. The document title appears near the top.
  2. Barcodes appear at many rotations and counts.
