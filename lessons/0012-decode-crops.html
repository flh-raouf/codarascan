Lesson 12

Decode Crops

Once boxes are good, decoding becomes a controlled crop problem.

Crop preparation

  1. Use the rotated quadrilateral, not just the axis-aligned rectangle.
  2. Perspective-warp the barcode into a flat horizontal crop.
  3. Add margin so quiet zones survive.
  4. Try grayscale, Otsu threshold, and adaptive threshold variants.
  5. Send the crop to ZXing, pyzbar, or OpenCV.
box points -> perspective transform -> normalized crop
normalized crop -> decoder -> value, type, quality

Important

An empty decode result is not proof that the box is wrong. It may mean blur, bad thresholding, clipped quiet zones, or unsupported symbology.

Retrieval check

Why add margin before decoding?

  1. To hide false positives.
  2. To preserve quiet zones.

Primary source: pyzbar documents decoded data, type, rectangle, polygon, orientation, and quality in its project page.
