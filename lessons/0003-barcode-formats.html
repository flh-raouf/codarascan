Barcode Formats

A detector does not need to know every symbology at first. A decoder does.

FamilyExamplesDetector clue
Retail 1DUPC-A, EAN-13, EAN-8Long parallel bars, often with human-readable digits.
Industrial 1DCode 128, Code 39, ITF, GS1-128Same barcode texture, often on logistics or part labels.
Stacked 2DPDF417Rows of tiny linear patterns stacked vertically.
Matrix 2DQR, Data Matrix, AztecGrid or square modules, not long bars.

For your dossier

The pasted automotive labels visually look like industrial 1D symbols. The exact format can wait until crop decoding. Localization can start with "find dense parallel bars."
