Lesson 7

Deterministic 1D Localization

The core trick: turn many thin parallel bars into one candidate rectangle.

The detector boxes the long header barcode and several rotated label barcodes. Some boxes need later cleanup.

Minimal pipeline

gray = grayscale(page)
grad = abs(sobel_x(gray))
mask = threshold(grad)
mask = close(mask, wide_kernel)
contours = find_contours(mask)
boxes = min_area_rect(contours)

Rotation strategy

If the detector expects vertical bars, rotate the page through a small angle set, detect there, then map the box points back. This is boring and effective.

Retrieval check

What does morphology closing do for 1D barcode localization?

  1. Merges nearby bars into blobs.
  2. Decodes the barcode string.

Primary source: The classical paper Barcode Detection with Morphological Operations and Clustering studies morphology-based barcode detection.
