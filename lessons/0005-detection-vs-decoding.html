Lesson 5

Detection vs Decoding

Detection answers "where is the code?" Decoding answers "what does it say?"

Detector output

A box or polygon: four points around the barcode region, plus maybe a confidence score.

Decoder output

A string, a symbology type, and sometimes quality or orientation.

Why we separate them

A decoder can fail even when the detector found the right box: blur, bad thresholding, tiny modules, crop margins, or wrong symbology. If we mix the two, we will not know whether localization or reading failed.

page image -> detector -> candidate boxes
candidate crop -> rectification -> decoder -> string or empty

Retrieval check

If a crop is correct but unreadable, which stage failed?

  1. Decoding or crop preparation.
  2. PDF page rendering only.
